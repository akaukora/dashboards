A state is judged at the end of each calendar year after the first play. Favorite: cumulative plays reached the line (and stays a favorite from then on). In rotation: played that year and the year before. Back again: played that year after a year of silence. Faded: not played that year. Only complete years are drawn, so the span is cut to what the youngest selected cohort has lived through. Data: scrobbles.csv, artist names as scrobbled.
