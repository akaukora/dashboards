Favorites: only artists that crossed the line. Arrival is the time from the first play to the play that crossed it. Stayed = no gap of the chosen length between plays since becoming a favorite, and a play in the last 12 months; faded for a while = such a gap, but a play in the last 12 months; faded out = nothing in the last 12 months. Early or late: measured from each artist's own first play, so recent cohorts take part as soon as their first years are over. A gap is S+ months between two plays, or from the last play to the latest scrobble, anywhere in the history — before or after becoming a favorite. "Now" means at least one play in the last 12 months. Whole story: a silent year is a complete calendar year with no plays after the first; "came back" means any play after one; a favorite is "only after coming back" when it crossed the line after its first silent year. Young cohorts have had less time to go silent or come back, so they lean toward "never silent". Year by year: a state at the end of each calendar year after the first play — favorite (cumulative plays reached the line, and it stays one), in rotation (played that year and the year before), back again (played after a silent year), faded (not played that year); only complete years are drawn, so the span is cut to what the youngest selected cohort has lived through. Data: scrobbles.csv, artist names as scrobbled.
