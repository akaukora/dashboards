Drop your Letterboxd export here

Everything happens in your browser — the files are never uploaded anywhere.

  1. On Letterboxd, open Settings → Data and click Export your data.
  2. Unzip the download. It holds diary.csv, reviews.csv, ratings.csv, watched.csv…
  3. Drop diary.csv here — and reviews.csv too, if you write reviews. Several files at once is fine.
Also accepts a films.csv in the format of Antti's dashboard. Your data is kept in this browser (nothing else) until you press Forget my data.
